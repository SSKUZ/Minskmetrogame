## Columns and correspondence

Thirteen distinct front compositions were isolated from the walk sequence: 133623, 133756, 133804, 133833, 133849, 133854, 133905, 133913, 133924, 133932, 133946, 134007, 134014. The 133955 close-up repeats the harvest/basket composition in 133946; 133649 and 133807 show adjacent faces and are not counted as additional column positions. This is an inventory of the documented front compositions, not a surveyed claim about the total number of motifs elsewhere on the station.

The owner's requested arrangement is implemented as **13 pairs / 26 columns**, two rows with identical longitudinal coordinates. Pair i on both sides always uses relief i. The lower two-strip wheat ornament and short top ornament are common to all columns. Unknown other faces repeat the documented front composition; the archive does not provide a complete four-face survey of every pair.

The decorations are opaque solid columns with actual relief surface meshes, not transparent columns or flat floating posters. Conservative depth is reconstructed from photographic contrast; no measured 3D scan is claimed. Each mesh has fine normal mapping and generated tangents. Physical octagonal rims and panel dividers preserve the silhouette and seams. Meshes and materials are shared between corresponding instances. Small marble feet and plain white plaster upper sections follow the photos.
