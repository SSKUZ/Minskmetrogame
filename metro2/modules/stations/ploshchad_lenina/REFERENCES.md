## Station Base and lower transfer

Station-local copies retain the 105 × 10 m island dimensions, datum 1.1805 m, track coordinates, gauge, contact rail equipment and four track connection markers. The original uninterrupted slab is omitted: four structural platform slabs surround a 4 × 12 m stairwell. No platform mesh or platform collider crosses the hole.

Two 18-step flights descend an inferred 5.40 m via a 1.20 m intermediate landing. Structural colliders follow the slabs and treads. Platform bypasses remain on both sides. Passenger markers identify the upper entrance, landing, lower connection and bypasses; these are integration anchors, not a passenger simulation. The short lower passage terminates at `TransferToVakzalnaya`; the unseen Vakzalnaya station and vestibules are not invented.

Dimensions and spacing are photo-scaled estimates rather than a measured survey. Sculpture depth is inferred from tones; the award and portrait are photo-derived shallow reliefs, not full sculpture scans. Advertising screens use neutral faces. No unphotographed murals are added.

Publication imports/exports a single Godot 4.3 Web station pack and updates menu/catalog support. Existing station and train packs are retained. No scene, game, browser, visual or performance tests.
