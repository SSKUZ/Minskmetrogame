# Metro 2.0 — reusable world modules and Roblox portability

## Canonical scenes
- Station Base: `res://scenes/StationBaseStatic.tscn`
- Uruchye: `res://scenes/stations/uruchye_station_module.tscn`
- Depot: `res://scenes/depot/depot_world_module.tscn`
- Existing route entry point remains `res://scenes/depot/surface_depot_v40.tscn`; legacy `route_station_v41.tscn` instances the canonical Uruchye module.
- Keep station/depot modules free of train, camera, HUD, route-controller, and global environment. Place them with an external Node3D transform. Keep local geometry in metres.

## Mandatory portability rule for all future assets
Every station, depot building, tunnel segment, track, train, and prop must remain reproducible as standalone geometry with stable object names, local transforms, scale, materials and texture references. Preserve editable source and a documented export route to a Roblox Studio-compatible mesh format (FBX or OBJ as appropriate). Godot GLB may be retained as interchange/source, but is not itself a guarantee of direct Roblox import.

## Procedural geometry export
Current depot and Uruchye geometry is generated by GDScript at runtime, including MultiMesh instances and procedural shader materials. A .tscn file alone is **not** a transferable mesh. Before claiming Roblox readiness, implement and validate an export/bake pipeline that:
1. Instantiates the module in Godot and waits for its procedural builders to finish.
2. Expands MultiMeshInstance3D transforms into actual mesh instances, preserving world/local transforms, including any SurfaceTool geometry.
3. Bakes or substitutes Godot-only ShaderMaterial effects into portable PBR textures/materials.
4. Exports meshes with stable names and explicit origin, axes, units and material/texture files; splits oversized assemblies into practical Roblox imports.
5. Imports a sample in Roblox Studio and checks orientation, dimensions, UVs, normals, materials and collision; rebuilds scripts, lights, particles and interactions in Luau/Roblox as needed.
6. Records exported asset version, source commit, limitations, and validation result. No Roblox-compatible export is claimed until these tests pass.

## Scope of this change
Structural extraction only. No geometry/material changes, no gameplay rewrites, no APK, no claim of a tested export. Run Godot scene-load and visual smoke tests before merging to the playable branch.
