## Completed scope

- Ordinary platform population remains 40–60, distributed over the platform.
- Random boarding intent is selected at spawn (60% individual probability;
  families share intent); the rest wait. No time-based removal to pass a test.
- Queue-aware reachable door choice uses all four cars. Each door works
  independently; exiting people have priority at that door.
- Exit choice favors nearby doors in the same car. Alighters rise at their own
  places and join the aisle before moving along it; artificial interior queue
  packing is removed. Natural walking speeds and full sit/rise clips remain.
- Existing people, families, seated/standing places, grips, motion response,
  identities between stations and LOD are preserved. No new detail/LOD mechanics.
- Exchange completion and physical door safety are independent signals. The
  close command waits for both. Already-entered people can continue to seats.
